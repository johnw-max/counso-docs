# Understand Knowledge

Knowledge gives an Agent sources to consult while preparing an answer. Sources can include material that your workspace makes available through its configured data areas. The Agent still needs clear instructions about how to use those sources.

## Knowledge is a source, not a conclusion

Knowledge can help an Agent find relevant context, but it does not guarantee that an answer is complete, current, or correct. A source may be missing, out of date, duplicated, or outside the Agent's access. Ask the Agent to identify the material it relied on when that matters.

Choose sources that match the task. A procedure may need the current policy; a review may need the source document and the relevant period; a summary may need the complete set rather than one convenient file. Keep unrelated material out of the Agent's scope so its answer is easier to check.

## Use Knowledge with care

Before relying on an answer, compare important statements with the source. Check dates, amounts, names, and conditions. If the source is incomplete, ask for the missing material or narrow the question. Do not treat a confident answer as proof that a source was available or that an external action was completed.
