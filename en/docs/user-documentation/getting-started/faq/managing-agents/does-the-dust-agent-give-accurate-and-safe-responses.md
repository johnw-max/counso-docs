# Are Agent responses always accurate and safe?

No. An Agent can misunderstand a request, miss relevant material, or state an unsupported fact confidently. Treat its output as a draft. Check important claims against the cited source, especially names, amounts, dates, and decisions.

Do not rely on an answer alone to approve a payment, post an accounting entry, make a legal or hiring decision, or send a customer commitment. Ask the Agent to separate evidence, assumptions, and open questions.
