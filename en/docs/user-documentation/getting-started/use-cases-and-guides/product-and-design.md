# Product and design use cases

Product and design teams can use Agents to review copy against a supplied style guide, summarise research, organise feedback, and prepare launch material. Keep a traceable link to the evidence behind product claims and customer themes.

## Review UX copy

Provide the current text, target user, interface context, and approved terminology. Ask for clarity, accessibility, consistency, and possible ambiguity. Request suggested alternatives with a short reason; the product designer makes the final choice.

## Prepare a release brief

Supply the approved feature notes, audience, availability, and known limitations. Ask the Agent to produce an internal summary, customer-facing draft, and a list of claims needing product or legal review. Never infer launch dates or availability beyond the source.

## Summarise user feedback

Choose a defined period and source set. Ask the Agent to group feedback by user need, provide representative citations, distinguish frequency from impact, and list contradictory evidence. Product researchers validate the sample before presenting a trend.

## Explore a market question

If web research is enabled, ask for dated sources and separate verified findings from hypotheses. Keep competitor or market claims linked to the original page and review them again before external use.

## Suggested request

```text
Review this settings-page copy for a first-time administrator. Flag unclear terms, accessibility concerns, and claims that are not supported by the supplied product notes. Suggest alternatives but do not change the source file.
```
