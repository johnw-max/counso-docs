# What AI models are available in Dust?

Dust gives you access to models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, and other providers, plus three **auto models**: **Basic**, **Standard**, and **Premium**. Each auto model is an ordered list of model and reasoning-effort combinations maintained by Dust. Dust periodically benchmarks and updates those lists, then uses the first available candidate on the selected list when you send a message.

Auto models do not choose a different model for each request based on the task. They give you a maintained choice at a defined capability and cost level, so you do not have to track individual models or update agents when the preferred model changes.

You choose a model in two places: the model picker in the composer, and **Advanced** > **Model selection** in Agent Builder.

For the current auto-model lists and guidance on which one to use, see [Auto models](/docs/user-documentation/agents/model-selection#auto-models).

Administrators can cap which models, reasoning efforts, and auto models each member can select. See [Model access tiers](/docs/user-documentation/admins/usage-seats-and-credits/model-access-tiers).

The exact list of available models changes as providers ship new models and as Dust updates its curated lists. The picker always shows what is currently available to you.
