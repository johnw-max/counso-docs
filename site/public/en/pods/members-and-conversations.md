# Manage Pod members and conversations

Members share a Pod's conversations, tasks, and files. Use the member list to establish the access boundary, then use conversations to keep decisions and progress visible to that group.

## Before you begin

Choose at least one Editor and decide which people need to see the full Pod history. Put internal notes and customer material in separate Pods when their audiences differ.

## Invite and manage members

1. Open the Pod's **Settings** and choose **Manage** in the Members area.
2. Search by name or email, select people, and choose **Set as editor** only for people who should manage the Pod.
3. Invite the selected members.
4. To change a role or remove someone, use the member menu and confirm the action.
5. Keep at least one Editor. A Pod cannot be left without an Editor.

Members can read conversations, create tasks, and upload files. Editors can also change membership, rename or describe the Pod, change visibility, archive it, and manage the pinned Frame.

## Start a shared conversation

1. Open **Conversations** and write a self-contained request.
2. Mention an agent with `@` when you want it to respond, and mention a teammate when you need their attention.
3. Include the relevant file, task, period, or decision in the message.
4. Follow the thread and record resulting work as a task when it has an owner and a next step.

Conversations created in the Pod are visible to members and become searchable Pod knowledge. Triggered conversations can be marked separately in the list. Each member can choose their own new-conversation and new-message notifications.

## What you should see afterward

An invited person can open the Pod according to its visibility and role. A shared conversation remains available to the team, and an agent can use prior Pod conversations when answering later questions.

## Common questions

### Can I invite someone to a Restricted Pod by mentioning them?

An Editor can use a mention to start the invite flow. The person does not gain access until the invite is accepted and the membership is established.

### How do I keep a customer from seeing internal work?

Use separate Pods with separate memberships. Hiding a conversation or file does not create an access boundary.

### Can a member leave when they are the only Editor?

No. Add another Editor first so the Pod remains manageable.
