# Introduction to Counso AI

Counso AI is an AI workspace for individuals and teams. Use available AI models, work with documents and knowledge sources, create and use Agents, connect business applications, and carry out supported workflows. What you can use depends on your workspace and role.

## Start at the application

Open [Counso AI](https://app.counso.ai). After you sign in, take a moment to identify the three main areas:

1. **Work** is where you start conversations and find Agents, Skills, Pods, and Conversations.
2. **Spaces** is where workspace data areas are organised.
3. **Admin** is where administrators manage workspace settings and permissions.

If you cannot see an area or Agent, ask your workspace administrator to check your access.

## Your first useful action

From **Work**, click the new conversation button (**Create a new conversation**). Check the selected agent, review the available model control, and describe one small task with the context the agent needs. Keep the first request easy to review, such as asking for a short summary of a document you are allowed to use. Before you share or rely on the answer, check the source and important facts yourself. Available models and tools depend on workspace configuration and permissions.

## What to learn next

Read [Workspace navigation](/en/getting-started/workspace-navigation/#workspace-navigation) to see where common work areas live. Then learn how your workspace uses Agents, Skills, Tools, Connections, and Spaces. If a workflow could change an external record, send a message, publish content, or create a charge, confirm the intended action and check the destination system afterwards.
